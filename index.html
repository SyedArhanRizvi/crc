About CRC Sector 150 Noida

CRC Sector 1 Gr. Noida West is a premium residential development located in one of Noida's greenest and fastest-growing sectors. Designed to offer a luxurious lifestyle, the project is expected to feature spacious 3 & 3.5 BHK residences with contemporary architecture, elegant interiors, and thoughtfully planned layouts. Residents will enjoy a wide range of world-class amenities, including a modern clubhouse, swimming pool, fitness center, landscaped gardens, jogging tracks, children's play area, sports facilities, and 24x7 security. Strategically positioned near the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and Yamuna Expressway, the project provides seamless connectivity to Delhi, Greater Noida, Jewar International Airport, IT hubs, educational institutions, healthcare centers, and entertainment destinations. Surrounded by lush greenery and open spaces, CRC Sector 1 Gr. Noida West offers the perfect balance of luxury, comfort, convenience, and long-term investment potential, making it an ideal choice for both homebuyers and investors.
